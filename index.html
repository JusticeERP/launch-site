Planned Product Capabilities

Built for Comprehensive Future Planning

Here is the full checklist of features engineered directly into JusticeERP to facilitate strategic discussions.

1. Real-Time Threaded Discussions

Embed conversations directly within strategic milestones and roadmap documents so stakeholders can debate future plans contextually.

2. Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

Granular permissions ensuring sensitive enterprise roadmap discussions remain secure between executives, legal counsel, and technical leads.

3. Live Presence & Cursors

See who is actively reviewing, commenting, or editing future plan scenarios in real-time across your organization.

4. Trajectory Parameter Modeling

Interactive inputs allowing leaders to test various strategic focus areas (e.g. Corporate Expansion, AI Workflow Automation, Firm Restructuring).

5. Instant AI Synthesis

Automated prediction engines that calculate success probability, projected valuation uplift, and potential compliance friction within seconds.

6. Risk & Reward Matrix

Quantitative analytics assessing the trade-offs of upcoming 3-to-5-year milestones with complete transparency.

7. Version-Controlled Future Plans

Track how your organization's strategy evolves over quarters with immutable audit logs and rollback options.

8. Exportable Executive Summaries

One-click generation of board-ready reports outlining the outcomes of your future plan discussions.

9. Enterprise Security & Compliance

End-to-end encryption combined with automated regulatory framework checks for ultimate peace of mind.
